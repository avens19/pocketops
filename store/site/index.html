PocketOps

Alerts, logs and traces from your SigNoz, on your phone.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

PocketOps is a read-only mobile client for SigNoz. Check what is firing, search logs, and follow a slow or failing request through your services without opening a laptop.

  • Alerts: every rule by state, with firing instances and a jump to the logs or traces behind them.
  • Logs: search, severity and service filters, live tail, structured JSON read properly.
  • Traces: errors and slowest views, group by any field, waterfall with a jump to the failure.
  • Services and metrics: calls, errors and p99, with charts you can scrub.

Connect with your SigNoz URL and a Viewer-role service-account API key. There is no account with us, no analytics and no advertising. See the privacy policy.
